I study how the physics and variability of emerging memory devices can guide the design of robust, efficient learning systems. I am pursuing a B.Eng. in Microelectronics at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), with graduation expected in July 2027. I plan to apply for PhD programs beginning in 2027.
