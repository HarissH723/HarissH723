I study how the physics and variability of emerging memory devices can guide the design of robust, efficient learning systems. I am pursuing a B.Eng. in Microelectronics at the Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), with graduation expected in July 2027. I plan to apply for PhD programs beginning in 2027.

### Research interests

`Memristive devices` `Neuromorphic hardware` `Analog in-memory computing` `Continual learning`

### Research at a glance

| 🔬 Devices and learning | ⚙️ Hardware and systems |

| **Memristive devices** · Resistive memory, device variability, and adaptive programming for class-incremental learning. | **Efficient computing** · Analog in-memory computing, neuromorphic hardware, and an FPGA-verified RV32I processor. |
